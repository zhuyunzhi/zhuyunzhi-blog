The long way round

I did not set out to write about trade. I set out to answer emails, quote jobs, and keep promises across a language I was still learning. The writing here is what I worked out along the way.

Based in Xiamen, China
Role Overseas marketing
Writes in English
Reply time 1–2 business days
Zhu Yunzhi
Export desk · Market entry · Content

For several years I have been the person in the middle: between a factory that measures success in millimetres and a buyer three thousand kilometres away who measures it in certainty.

Most of that time was unglamorous. Quotation sheets. Sample logistics. Following up for the fourth time on a question that had already been answered. But the pattern that emerged was consistent, and it was not about price: the suppliers who grew were the ones whose customers were never surprised.

I write here because that lesson is badly documented. Sales advice is everywhere; the operational reality of keeping a promise across a language barrier, a time zone and a customs desk is not.

These notes are personal. They are not a company blog, and nothing here speaks for anyone but me.
